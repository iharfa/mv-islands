    Miladhunmadulu Uthuruburi (Shaviyani Atoll)

    Miladhunmadulu Uthuruburi Atoll (Northern Miladhunmadulu Atoll), also known by the abbreviated name Shaviyani Atoll,[1] is an Administrative division of the Maldives. It corresponds to the northern section of the natural Miladhunmadulu Atoll,[2] located in the north of the Maldives.
