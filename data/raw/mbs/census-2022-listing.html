Term Definition
Resident Population Population who have been living in Maldives for more than one year or intends to live in Maldives for 1 year or more. This includes Resident Maldivian Population and Resident Foreign Population
Resident Maldivian Maldivian who have been living in Maldives for more than one year or intends to live in Maldives for 1 year or more. Maldivians who are permanently living abroad is excluded from here
Resident Foreigners Foreigners who have living in Maldives for more than one year or intends to live in Maldives for 1 year or more. Foreigners such as tourists, short term stay is excluded from this count
Maale Includes the 4 wards of Maale (Henveiru, Galolhu, Machchangoalhi, Maafannu), Hulumaale, Villimaale, and K.Hulhule with harbour of these areas
Atolls Includes administrative islands and non-administrative islands
Administrative islands (Admin islands) These are islands with islands/city councils
Non-administrative islands (Non-admin islands) Includes resorts and industrial islands and other islands. Industrial islands and other islands includes resorts under development, islands leased out for agricultural , fisheries purpose, etc. These islands are included if anyone lives and sleep there. It also includes Thilafushi and Gulheefalhu
South Thiladhunmathi (HDh Atoll) The count of population in South Thiladhunmathi is including Kulhudhuffushi City
Maale Atoll (K) Maale Atoll includes only administrative islands within the atoll and excludes Maale (Maale’, Villimaale, Hulhumaale, Hulhule)
Place of enumeration Place of enumeration refers to the island where the person was found on census night (13 September 00.00 hrs). The migration tables however excludes the non-resident Maldivians who were enumerated in the Maldives
Place of usual residence The island where the person resided at the time of census. Usual residence is determined based on the duration of one year or more. If the person has lived in that island for less than one year, but intends to live for one year, that island is considered as the usual residence of the person
Place of registration The island where the person is registered at the time of Census. By law, all Maldivians are required to register in a house and upon which their national identity card can be issued
Place of previous residence The island/country where the person has lived for 1 year or more before moving to the place of usual residence. For people who have lived in many different islands/countries over the past, this is the island/country where the person lived immediately prior to moving to the place of usual residence
Migrant A person who have ever changed his/her island of usual residence. In other words, if a person has resided for 1 year or more on another island or country other than his/her current usual place of residence, then that person is considered as a migrant. In cases where the duration is less than 1 year, then his/her or intention is considerered
Non-migrant A person who has never changed his/her island of usual residence. Those who have never been away from their island of usual residence or never lived in another country for longer than 1 year
Place of birth Island/country where the person was born

Provisional Results

This release presents the provisional results of Census 2022. It contains information on the population by basic demographic characteristics such as age , sex, nationality population distribution and household size. References will be made to Resident Population, resident Maldivian population and Foreign population at Republic, Male’ and Atolls level and refer to definitions used in the tables and summary write up
