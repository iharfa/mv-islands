Summary Description

Maafushi is one of the most prominent inhabited islands in the Kaafu Atoll. Historically known for its high-security prison facility, the island underwent a major economic transformation following the introduction of local tourism in 2010. Today, it serves as a primary hub for guesthouse-based tourism in the Maldives. Geographically, the island is elongated and sits on the eastern rim of the South Malé Atoll, protected by an extensive reef system on its eastern seaward side.
